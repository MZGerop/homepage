Patch 18.6 · What changed? 05 Nov 2026

Buffs: Blossom 7/9, Sprykin ranged BFF 5/7, Nidalee, Sentinel shields, Malphite, Kobuko, Rek'Sai and Murkwolf. Nerf: Elderwood 9 bonus HP. Rammus received no direct buff; Sprykin teams are the interesting change.

Watch: Riftbeast Elder Dragon, Elder Dragon + Draven, Sprykin Rammus + Azir, Invoker Ahri + Morgana, and Blossom Master Yi. Strong options, not builds to force when contested.

03 · Build it right

Items & component combinations

Universal items first. The best item depends on champion ability, your frontline, and the enemy board.

Quick item builder

+
Easy starter sets: AD auto-attacker: Guinsoo / Infinity Edge / Last Whisper. AP caster: Shojin / Jeweled Gauntlet / Deathcap. Main tank: Gargoyle / Warmog / Spirit Visage. These are categories, not fixed best-in-slot for every champion.
Against an unkillable Rammus: use Last Whisper for physical damage, Void Staff for magic damage, Giant Slayer versus high HP, and anti-heal when needed. Attack speed alone cannot shred armor.

05 · Get first place

Endgame winning checklist

Use this during stages 4–6, not just after a loss.

Spot the actual win condition

  • Vertical: reach a meaningful trait threshold (e.g. 7 Riftbeast, 5/7 Elderwood) rather than adding random trait counts.
  • Horizontal: combine individually strong units and useful smaller trait bonuses.
  • Secondary carry: another threat makes your board less dependent on one champion.
  • Positioning: scout your final opponents and move your carry / tanks to improve matchups.
  • Spending: when the next loss eliminates you, unused gold generally has no value.
Important: A 3★ 4-cost is a huge win condition; do not confuse it with a 3★ 1-cost. You usually cannot chase both a 3★ expensive carry and a fast Level 9 without unusual economy.
